Design to spec

            

              
This is where the tool sizes the
              circuit for you. Type the numbers it must meet, then either
              generate a design from the specs (a physics-based starting
              point, refined by search) or optimize from the values already
              on the page. Every step of the search is a real ngspice run,
              so the SKY130 circuits take minutes, not seconds, and the
              winning sizing can be applied to the page with one click.

Step response

            

              
A sweep measures the amplifier being polite.
              This asks how fast it can actually move: the same circuit as a unity
              buffer, hit with a 0.3 V step, up and back down. One transient run,
              about twenty seconds.

Rejection and range

            

              
The rest of the datasheet: how well the
              amplifier ignores what both inputs do together and what its supply
              does, and how far its input and output can travel before it stops
              working. Four copies of the circuit in one run, about half a minute.

Layout

            

              
A schematic pretends a wire is free and a
              transistor takes no room. This places the devices in a row at the
              foundry's own minimum spacing, reports what that costs in silicon,
              and hangs the interconnect it implies back on the circuit to
              measure what it costs in margin. Two simulations, about half a
              minute.

              
Two questions get answered. The geometry
              is checked against thirty-two rules read from the PDK, and the
              drawing is compared against the netlist that was simulated, so a
              wire on the wrong pin is caught rather than admired. Neither is
              sign-off: the real rule deck runs to thousands and a real layout
              versus schematic needs Netgen. The placer is a row and the router
              is one track a net, which is not what anyone would call
              placement or routing.

    Pinned numbers
    
                
    A pin freezes a sizing and what it

    Robustness

              rerun the same circuit across process corners, supply and temperature, or
              across mismatch samples. Every row is a full simulation; expect minutes.
