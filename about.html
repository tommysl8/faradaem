About

What this is and how it works.

What Faradaem is

Faradaem is an AI-assisted analog IC design tool. You give it a specification — gain, bandwidth, phase margin, power budget — and it returns a verified transistor-level design.

Verified means the numbers were measured, not asserted. Every value Faradaem reports about a circuit was produced by simulating that circuit.

How it works

The work is split three ways so each part does only what it is good at.

ngspice is the sole source of numerical truth. Every operating point, gain and margin comes out of a real simulator run. Nothing else in the system is permitted to produce a circuit value.

A numerical optimizer does the search through component and bias space. It is the part that actually finds sizings that meet a spec, because that is a numerical problem and numerical methods solve it well.

An LLM handles strategy only: choosing a topology, framing trade-offs, deciding what to try next, and explaining the result. It never computes a circuit value. When you see a voltage on this site, a simulator measured it.
